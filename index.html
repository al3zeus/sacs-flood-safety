Before During After

Before a Flood

Check Weather Forecasts

Monitor weather updates regularly to prepare for possible flooding.

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Pack food, water, medicine, flashlight, and important documents.

Move Valuables Higher

Place important items on higher ground to avoid water damage.

During a Flood

Stay on Higher Ground

Move to safe, elevated areas and avoid floodwaters.

Follow News Updates

Stay informed through official alerts and emergency announcements.

Keep Phone Ready

Charge your phone and keep emergency contacts available.

After a Flood

Inspect Damage

Check your home and belongings for damage before returning.

Clean and Disinfect

Clean your home thoroughly to prevent disease and contamination.

Help Others

Support neighbors and community members after the disaster.
